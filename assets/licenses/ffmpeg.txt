VidCtrl converts videos with FFmpeg (https://ffmpeg.org), which it runs as
two separate programs, ffmpeg and ffprobe. The copies that come with VidCtrl
are builds of FFmpeg 8.1 from its unmodified source. They include
libraries such as x264, x265, SVT-AV1, LAME, zimg and libvmaf, and are
licensed under the GNU General Public License, version 3, whose text
follows.

Where they and their source code come from:

FFmpeg 8.1.3, built for VidCtrl (release 8.1.3-3), the same for macOS,
Linux and Windows.
  The build script, which lists the version of every library:
    https://github.com/koorchik/vidctrl-ffmpeg
  The builds, and beside them the source of every part:
    https://github.com/koorchik/vidctrl-ffmpeg/releases/tag/8.1.3-3
  FFmpeg's source: https://ffmpeg.org/releases/ffmpeg-8.1.3.tar.xz
